Privacy Policy for Fraud Guardian

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 | Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Fraud Guardian ("we", "our", or "the App") is an on-device cybersecurity solution built to protect individuals, families, and senior citizens across India from deceptive messaging scams, digital arrest extortion, fake banking trojan APK droppers, and reverse UPI PIN fraud.

Core Privacy Pledge: Fraud Guardian operates on a Zero-Knowledge Architecture. We do not read, log, record, store, or transmit your private chats, personal SMS messages, contacts, photos, or personal identifiers to any external server. All threat evaluations occur 100% locally on your device in volatile memory (RAM).

1. Regulatory Compliance (Pan-India)

This Privacy Policy is formulated in strict accordance with:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023: Principle of data minimization and purpose limitation.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 & 2008 Amendments: Including Section 79 Intermediary Guidelines.
  • RBI Cyber Security Framework for Banks: Consumer advisory alignment against unauthorized UPI pull requests and sideloaded APK trojans.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Accordingly:

  • Personal Communications: We DO NOT collect or upload chat messages, text content, phone numbers, or conversation histories.
  • Device & Identity Information: We DO NOT collect IMEI, Advertising IDs, MAC addresses, or location data.
  • Account Creation: Fraud Guardian requires NO user account, login credentials, or email address to activate core protection.
  • Family Circle Data: Optional contact nicknames and phone numbers entered into the "Family Circle" tab remain encrypted exclusively inside Android's private EncryptedSharedPreferences on your local handset.

3. Android Permissions & Justifications

To provide real-time protection, Fraud Guardian requests specific permissions strictly required for threat detection:

  • Notification Access (android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE):
    Purpose: Allows the on-device scanner to inspect incoming notifications from messaging applications (e.g., WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram) for malicious web links (URLs), reverse UPI payment triggers, or Trojan APK file references.
    Execution: All text inspection happens in-memory (<5 milliseconds) using regex heuristics. Non-threatening notifications are immediately discarded from RAM without persistence.
  • Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW):
    Purpose: Enables the application to instantly display an emergency high-priority warning modal over WhatsApp or your messaging screen when a critical banking trojan or digital arrest extortion attempt is intercepted.
  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
    Purpose: Delivers critical security threat alerts to the Android notification shade when an unauthorized link or file download is blocked.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Fraud Guardian maintains an optional local "Intercepted Threat Log" solely within the device's sandbox. Users may clear this log at any time with a single tap. Uninstalling the application immediately and irreversibly deletes all stored local logs.

5. Third-Party Disclosures & Cloud Services

Fraud Guardian does not share, sell, rent, or trade personal data with advertisers, third-party analytics SDKs, data brokers, or marketing networks. All rule evaluation is completely client-side.

6. 1930 Cyber Crime Helpline & Legal Assistance

Fraud Guardian provides convenient 1-tap dialers and incident dossier generators for the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930), cybercrime.gov.in, and Bank Fraud Control Units (FCUs). Generating a dossier formats evidence strictly on your screen for your own use; Fraud Guardian never files reports or transmits telemetry without your explicit instruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Fraud Guardian does not collect personal information from individuals of any age, including children under 18.
